Tab & Type privacy policy
Effective date: October 30, 2026

This extension does not collect analytics, telemetry, browsing history, or
personal data for its developers. It has no developer-operated service and
does not send your saved settings to a server.

Your selected start-page address is stored locally using Firefox's extension
storage. The extension requests access to the website you choose so it can
handle links within that embedded page. It does not request access to your
bookmarks, passwords, clipboard, or browsing history.

To restore your place when you use Back or Forward, the extension temporarily
keeps the most recent start-page URL, standard tab selections, open/closed
section states, element identifiers/paths and scroll positions for each browser
tab in memory. It does not retain page HTML, form entries or
arbitrary website application state. These view snapshots are not sent anywhere
or written to extension storage. They are cleared when the tab closes, the
selected site changes, access is revoked, or the extension stops running.

When you open a new tab, Firefox loads your chosen website. That website and
its resources make their ordinary network requests and may use cookies,
analytics, or other data collection under the website's own policies. When
you click a link, Firefox loads the destination you chose. Those website
requests are separate from the extension's own data practices.

You can change the selected website in the extension's settings and revoke
its website access through Firefox's add-on permissions. The extension
requests only the host access needed for your selected website and releases
the previous host's permission when you save a different host.
