Tab & Type

Show a website when you open a new tab. Ordinary links open in the full tab, and Firefox’s address bar stays ready when the tab first opens.

Use the final address shown after the site loads, including https:// and www if present.

Firefox will ask for access to the website you choose so this add-on can open its links correctly. Your choice stays on this device.

Some sites do not allow embedded pages. Their protections remain in place. Address-bar searches use your existing Firefox search engine.
